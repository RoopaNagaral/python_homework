#Task 5: Ethical Web Scraping
1. Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?
- Spam blacklist pages such as /wiki/MediaWiki:Spam-blacklist and related talk pages.
- Deletion-related pages including Articles for deletion, Votes for deletion, Pages for deletion, Miscellany for deletion, Categories for discussion, and their talk pages.

2. Are there specific rules for certain user agents?
- Many bots are fully disallowed, including MJ12bot, Mediapartners-Google*, UbiCrawler, DOC, Zao, Zealbot, MSIECrawler, HTTrack, WebReaper, NPBot, wget, and others.
- SemrushBot has a crawl-delay of 5 seconds.

3. Reflection:
Websites use robots.txt to control how automated crawlers access their content, protecting sensitive or high-traffic areas from being overloaded. 
These rules help ensure that scraping is done responsibly and respectfully. 
Robots.txt promotes ethical scraping by providing clear guidelines that crawlers should follow.
